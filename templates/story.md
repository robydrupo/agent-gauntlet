## What I want

Describe what you want in your own words. Informal is fine; the Specifier's job is to make
it precise. Say what should happen, not how to build it.

Cover the ordinary case first. Then say what should happen when things are awkward: nothing
supplied, far too much supplied, a value that makes no sense. If you'd rather the system
refuse than guess, say so — that is a requirement, and the Specifier will turn it into a
scenario.

## How I'd check it myself

way a user would. This becomes the QA procedure, and then an executable script.
